# Xi Role Priors

Use these as optional interpretation aids when uploaded materials already suggest them.

These are priors, not mandatory labels.

## xiCurate
Normalization, extraction, deduplication, record shaping, or schema cleanup.

## xiAudit
Validation, drift detection, rule checking, anomaly surfacing, or quality review.

## xiProvenance
Hashing, receipts, lineage, source linkage, or evidence preservation.

## Prism
Refraction, scoring, adjudication, or boundary-state emission.

## Archivist
Long-horizon record keeping, drift memory, historical continuity, or postmortem support.

## Goldmine
High-level orchestration across generators, observers, teachers, or student pools.

## Use rules

- Prefer a generic seam class when evidence for the named role is weak.
- Surface the named role only when the material itself uses the role or strongly implies the same function.
- A bundle may contain multiple xi-like roles. Do not force one role to absorb the others.
