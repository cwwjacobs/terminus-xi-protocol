# XI Lineage

The XI line predates Terminus XI Protocol and appears in the ixC Dataset Curation Stack and related XI materials.

Historical roles include xiProvenanceAgent, xiAuditAgent, xiCurateAgent, Archivist, Artifactor, and Prism. Historical material establishes a separation between capability, deterministic governance wrappers, and charter/law, plus the fixed rule that no unvalidated artifact may cross a stage boundary.

## Preserved evidence

Two source streams are retained under `archive/`:

1. `xi-materials-audit/` — selected readable evidence from the 2026-08-28 XI preservation/audit bundle. The raw bundle itself is intentionally not committed.
2. `ixc-dataset-curation-stack-c50ad56/` — a minimal source snapshot of the historical private repository at commit `c50ad56`.

See `archive/SOURCE_MANIFEST.json` for cryptographic identities and source references.

## Canonicalization policy

Historical presence is not canonical authority. Contradictory versions, malformed copies, incomplete charter implementations, and duplicated agents were observed in recovered material. History is evidence to be re-admitted, not code to be blindly inherited.
